# Code of Conduct for PranaCode+

## Our Commitment

We are committed to creating a welcoming, safe, and inclusive environment
for everyone contributing to PranaCode+. This project is built on respect,
collaboration, and creativity.

All contributors, maintainers, and participants agree to uphold this Code
of Conduct.

---

## Expected Behavior

Participants should:
• Be respectful and constructive  
• Use inclusive language  
• Welcome diverse viewpoints  
• Accept feedback gracefully  
• Focus on collaboration and problem‑solving  

---

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following are not tolerated:
• Harassment or discrimination  
• Personal attacks or insults  
• Threatening or violent language  
• Sharing private information  
• Disruptive or hostile behavior  

---

## Reporting Issues

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior:
• Open a private issue  
• Contact the repository owner (Krystal Nymann) directly  

All reports will be handled respectfully and confidentially.

---

## Enforcement

Violations may result in:
• warnings  
• temporary bans  
• permanent removal from the project  

---

## Acknowledgment

This Code of Conduct is inspired by the Contributor Covenant and adapted
for the PranaCode+ ecosystem.
